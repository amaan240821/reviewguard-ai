# Report assets

All figures generated by `make evaluate` at 300 DPI.

## 01_dataset_distribution

`reports/figures/01_dataset_distribution.png`

After removing four duplicated texts, the corpus contains 1,596 unique reviews: 800 deceptive and 796 truthful.

## 02_suspicion_distribution

`reports/figures/02_suspicion_distribution.png`

Design-weight suspicion scores on the untouched 320-review holdout. Scores are screening indicators, not calibrated fraud probabilities.

## 03_scores_by_label

`reports/figures/03_scores_by_label.png`

Score distributions overlap; composite signals may reduce binary classification performance because they measure additional phenomena.

## 04_confusion_matrix

`reports/figures/04_confusion_matrix.png`

Classifier at the frozen training-CV threshold: TN=143, FP=17, FN=11, TP=149.

## 05_sentiment_distribution

`reports/figures/05_sentiment_distribution.png`

Predicted sentiment on the holdout using distilbert. Sentiment labels are not deception labels.

## 06_similarity_network

`reports/figures/06_similarity_network.png`

Synthetic motivating example only. Similarity edges are evidence of shared wording, not proof of coordinated authorship.

## 07_roc_curve

`reports/figures/07_roc_curve.png`

Holdout classifier ROC-AUC 0.9640; composite ROC-AUC 0.9418.

## 08_precision_recall

`reports/figures/08_precision_recall.png`

Classifier average precision 0.9628; positive class is deceptive.

## 09_signal_importance

`reports/figures/09_signal_importance.png`

Mean raw signal strength on the holdout, not causal importance. Rating is absent in this corpus.

## 10_embedding_projection

`reports/figures/10_embedding_projection.png`

Seeded t-SNE projection of holdout embeddings. Distances in this visualization are exploratory, not a classifier.
